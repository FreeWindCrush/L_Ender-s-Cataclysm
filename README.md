## 📖 What Is L_Ender's Cataclysm?

**L_Ender's Cataclysm** is a combat-focused mod that transforms Minecraft into a network of epic dungeons, deadly bosses, and powerful loot. It adds handcrafted structures across the Overworld, Nether, and End — each hiding unique encounters designed for players who enjoy demanding endgame challenges.

The mod's bosses are inspired by *Minecraft Dungeons* and other action games, featuring intricate attack patterns, phase transitions, and mechanics that punish button-mashing. You can't just tank through these fights — you need to learn the moveset and execute.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **8+ major bosses** — each with unique mechanics and arenas

- ✅ **Powerful gear** — weapons and armor that surpass netherite
- ✅ **Souls-like combat** — dodge, learn, execute
- ✅ **Multi-dimensional** — Overworld, Nether, and End content

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What is L_Ender's Cataclysm?**
A: A combat mod that adds difficult dungeons, challenging bosses, and powerful gear inspired by *Minecraft Dungeons* and action games.

**Q: Is L_Ender's Cataclysm free?**
A: Yes — 100% free.

**Q: What Minecraft versions are supported?**
A: **1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1, and 1.21.1/1.21.5**. Forge for older versions, NeoForge for 1.21+.

**Q: Forge, NeoForge, or Fabric?**
A: **Forge and NeoForge only.** No Fabric support.

**Q: What's the boss order?**
A: Start with dungeon mini-bosses and The Harbinger around late-diamond gear. Netherite Monstrosity and Ignis need netherite. Save End and pyramid bosses for last.

**Q: Why does my damage seem capped?**
A: Many bosses limit per-hit damage to prevent one-shot builds. Sustained DPS and mechanic execution win fights.

**Q: How do I find dungeons?**
A: Ancient Factories are deep underground in the Overworld. Burning Arena and Soul Black Smith are in the Nether. Ruined Citadel is in the End. Use a new world for best results.

**Q: What's the best gear for bosses?**
A: Start with diamond armor. Netherite is required for mid-tier bosses. Ignitium and Witherite armor are endgame.

**Q: Does it work with OptiFine?**
A: No — Cataclysm has texture issues with OptiFine. Use Sodium/Iris instead.

**Q: Does it work on Bedrock / mobile?**
A: No — **Java Edition only**.

**Q: Can I use it with other mods?**

**Q: How do I uninstall it?**
